Mat2Seg: An RGB-NIR Dataset and Benchmark for Material Segmentation of Household Objects

RGB-NIR material segmentation for household objects.

Overview

Mat2Seg is an RGB-NIR dataset and benchmark for material segmentation of household objects. It combines visible and near-infrared imagery to study how complementary sensing can improve the delineation of material regions.

The benchmark focuses on material boundaries within everyday object scenes, supporting the evaluation of methods that reason about materials at the pixel level rather than assigning a single label to an entire image.

Task Material segmentation
Sensing modalities RGB and NIR
Focus Household objects and material boundaries

Paired RGB-NIR Capture

RGB

Visible-spectrum color, texture, and appearance cues from everyday object scenes.

NIR

Near-infrared observations that offer complementary signatures for distinguishing material regions.

Benchmark

01

Material regions

Evaluate pixel-level delineation of different materials within a shared household scene.

02

Multimodal comparison

03

Household contexts

Study material boundaries across familiar objects, surfaces, and mixed-material arrangements.
